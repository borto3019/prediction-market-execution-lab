Q2 Is the binary mispriced relative to a spot-implied model?

Every candidate above that failed forward traded a gap between the Kalshi price and a barrier probability implied by Kraken spot, strike, time to expiry and trailing realised volatility. The direct test asks which of the two forecast settlement better, on prospective entries neither was fitted to (first entry per contract, chronological 60/40 split for the recalibration).

Q3 How fast does Kalshi absorb spot moves?

Event study on the public tick sample. Event: a Kraken five-second log move of at least three trailing-vol standard deviations. Response: change in the Kalshi two-sided trade-implied mid, divided by the change the barrier model implies. 100% means fully priced. Bands are 95% bootstrap CIs over asset-day blocks.

Pass-through of the model-implied move

h = 0 is the end of the five-second spot window, so the value there is repricing in the same seconds as the spot move. The response then flattens below 100% everywhere except in the final three minutes. The shortfall does not close, so it reads as the spot model overstating the move (Q2) rather than as delay.

Is what remains tradable? Preregistered rules on the four held-out test days: buy the side the move indicates at the first observed print on that side after a reaction latency, hold to settlement, one contract, one entry per contract and side. Other families: follow signed taker flow (Q4) and trade the largest model-vs-mid gaps (Q2). All 22 specifications are shown. P-values are Holm-adjusted across all 22.

Q4 Does order flow predict the next quote or the outcome?

Signed Kalshi taker flow, (YES-taker − NO-taker contracts) / total over the trailing 30 s, sampled every 5 s. It is a channel the 181-feature book panel never used. Flow-sorted deciles of the next 30 s mid change and of the settlement surprise (outcome − mid). Flow barely moves the next quote. Settlement leans against heavy flow, a pattern that does not survive multiple-testing correction. Following the flow at the first print after 1 s loses on all eight preregistered variants.

Methodology and data

Live collection
Full-depth Kalshi and Kraken order books every 5 s for BTC and ETH, plus every Kalshi print with its venue trade_id, aligned with backward-only as-of joins (an observation is never matched to reference data recorded after it). Fail-closed collection validation and gap classification, e.g. the Thursday Kalshi maintenance window.
Research panel
— of BTC, — contracts, 181 engineered features. — (— BTC specifications attempted, — completed; — across both assets in the HF architecture.)
Search discipline
Hypotheses are written as mechanism memos and catalogue-hashed before scoring. Evaluation is nested walk-forward out-of-fold, then a global reality-check adjustment over the whole search; the latest cycle's adjusted p-values are 1.00 (BTC) and 0.997 (ETH). Blind base-rate gates check that every hypothesis can fire before it is frozen.
Execution model
Aggressive entries only: the walked 10-contract VWAP against the visible book, the canonical Kalshi taker fee ceil(0.07·q·p·(1−p)) cents, held to settlement. Passive fills are not assumed. A separate preregistered queue-model study found adverse selection offsets the cost saving. In the public tick sample (Q3–Q4) the entry price is the first observed print on the traded side after the latency; depth is not observed there.
Forward governance
Zero-start ledgers, frozen specifications, review checkpoints opened on blind evidence counts (trades, contracts, days, hour and weekday coverage), and a frozen mechanical suspension policy. Four of six candidates have met it.

BTC contracts carry about 38,000 Kalshi prints each, so a time-budgeted fetch reached 544 of 953 BTC contracts. They were taken in a seeded, outcome-blind random order that spans all ten days. Missing contracts are excluded, never filled. Spot is observed only when Kraken trades, so the latest spot print is often older than 1 s.

Every result is simulated paper execution against recorded or live public books. No real orders are submitted and nothing here is investment advice. Strategy conditions, thresholds, entry side and entry window are withheld.
