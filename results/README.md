# Results

Sanitized aggregate results. Every figure here is **simulated execution against
recorded order books** — no real orders have been placed.

## Included

**Multiple-testing outcome (BTC).** 2,410 specifications attempted, 2,236
evaluated cleanly, none clearing the pre-registered promotion threshold in any
cycle. The headline result of the project, and a null.

**Panel scale.** 1.90M synchronized cross-venue observations, 181 engineered
features, 71-day BTC panel over 6,645 contracts at 5-second resolution.

**Paper-forward monitoring (ETH).** Two candidates under prospective evaluation
against a frozen specification, with mechanical suspension rules. Live view in
the dashboard.

## Deliberately not included

Strategy parameters and thresholds, candidate catalogues, per-specification
search output, raw order books and fills, and the governance internals that would
let the search be reconstructed. These are what makes the result reproducible for
the author and are exactly what should not be public.

## Reading the numbers

Specification counts are search effort, not evidence. A large search producing no
survivor is the expected outcome of a correctly-specified multiple-testing
procedure; it is reported because suppressing it would misrepresent the method.
